01 / Colour

A strict, monochrome palette.

65% black and dark shades, 30% white and off-white, 5% champagne accent. Tailwind's default colours are disabled, so only these exist.

  • Primary Black

    #080808 · bg-ink

    Main dark surfaces, header over hero.

  • Secondary Black

    #171717 · bg-ink-2

    Alternate sections, solid header.

  • Dark Grey

    #252525 · bg-graphite

    Raised dark areas, body text on light.

  • Champagne

    #A88B60 · champagne

    Rules, numerals, focus, active states. ≤5%.

  • Off White

    #F5F5F2 · bg-bone

    Light sections, text on dark.

  • Pure White

    #FFFFFF · white

    Headings on dark, primary buttons.

  • Secondary Text

    #777777 · mute

    Large text (≥18px) and input lines on light only.

  • Border

    #E5E5E5 · line

    Hairlines on light surfaces.

Contrast rules

Measured against WCAG 2.2 AA. These rules are built into the surface system, so components follow them automatically.
